# Targeted Feedback Request

Please review the [report draft](report-draft.html) and [KT preview](dashboard-preview.md) with the following questions in mind:

1. **Correctness:** Does the 2016–2024 Table 1 window, the exclusion of partial-year Nunavut 2023, and the matched-year overall summaries for Yukon and the Northwest Territories make sense? Are the cell-specific `n` values and treatment of suppressed values and zero denominators correct?
2. **Interpretation:** Is the 2016 versus 2024 Canada endpoint comparison appropriately qualified? Does any wording invite a causal, age-adjusted, or service-performance interpretation that the data cannot support?
3. **Visual clarity:** Is the Table 1 easy to scan, and is its caption sufficient to explain the unequal observation counts? Can you distinguish a reported zero, a missing value, and a flagged 2025 point in both figures?
4. **Reproducibility:** Can you render `report-draft.qmd` from the repository root and regenerate the figures and CSV outputs using the documented packages and fixed CSV? What step is unclear?
5. **Audience fit:** Would a provincial or regional public-health reader know what to take from this preview and what decision still requires other evidence? Which one figure or caution deserves the most prominent position in the final KT product?
