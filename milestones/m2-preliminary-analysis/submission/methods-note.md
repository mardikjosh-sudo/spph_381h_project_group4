# Methods

We analyzed the Public Health Agency of Canada's public file (`SubstanceHarmsData.csv`), selecting annual opioid-death crude rates (overall and by sex) for 2016-2025. Of 33,250 source records, 420 rate records formed 140 region-years across 14 regions (13 provinces and territories plus Canada's national series). Before exclusions, the overall rate was missing for 6 region-years (4 suppressed, 2 unavailable) and each sex-specific rate for 25 (23 suppressed, 2 unavailable). The male-to-female ratio, calculated only when both sex-specific rates were reported and the female rate was positive, was undefined for 29 region-years (23 suppressed, 2 unavailable, 4 with a zero female rate). Suppressed and unavailable values stayed missing, and reported zeros were retained. Four region-years were excluded from summaries, leaving 136: Nunavut 2023 (partial year) and the 2025 values for British Columbia, Quebec, and Canada (reporting changes).

Table 1 reports the eligible, numeric, and missing years, the calendar years used, and the mean (sample SD) for each region and measure; these are arithmetic summaries, not pooled-period rates. For Yukon and the Northwest Territories, overall means use only years with both male and female rates reported, aligning their overall and sex-specific summaries. Their original overall means used 10 years, compared with only 5 and 6 years for the sex-specific means, respectively; averaging over different years can make the overall mean appear lower than both sex-specific means. Prince Edward Island's overall series is summarized independently because it is reported even though both sex-specific series are suppressed. An overall rate is published separately and does not reveal the suppressed sex-specific values.

The 2016, 2024, and 2025 endpoint comparison complements the annual figures but does not describe the intervening trajectory. The 2025 rates for British Columbia, Quebec, and Canada are flagged for reporting changes. Figure 1 shows annual overall rates for each region against Canada, and Figure 2 shows annual male-to-female ratios. Nunavut 2023 is omitted from the figures. Reported zeroes, such as Nunavut's 2016-2018 rates, are retained as zero; suppressed or unavailable values are not zero and are not imputed.

These summaries are descriptive and do not establish causal effects. Rates are crude, not age-adjusted, so differences between regions may partly reflect age structure. Suppression of small counts leaves the sex-specific series for Yukon and the Northwest Territories incomplete (5 and 6 of 10 years). Their overall means are restricted to those matched years; the table lists the years used. Nunavut's full-year reported values are zeroes for 2016–2018; 2019–2022 are suppressed, 2023 has partial-year coverage, and 2024–2025 are unavailable, so we do not interpret its mean. Prince Edward Island has no ratio because its sex-specific values are suppressed; a missing ratio does not imply equal rates.
