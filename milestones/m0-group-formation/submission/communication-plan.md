# Communication Plan

- **Primary channel:** Our Instagram group chat (all members already added). Day-to-day questions and updates go here.
- **Response window:** Within 3-5 hours. If you're busy and can't reply in that time, tell the group as soon as possible.
- **Weekly check-in:** A short meeting at the end of each lecture to assign tasks for the coming week. Each member confirms their tasks in the chat afterward. Seek to update other members on progress by Friday.
- **Decision record:** Decisions are discussed and announced on Instagram, then one member adds each dated decision to our GitHub workspace (README or `decisions.md`). Another member confirms the entry.
